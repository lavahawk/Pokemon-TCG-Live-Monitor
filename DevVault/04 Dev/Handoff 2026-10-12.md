### Statistics — [[deck_score]] (read this note for the maths)
- **Audit result:** the old "Bayes WR" was genuinely Bayesian but overconfident. Weighted meta W/L exceeded the real game counts, games are clustered by pilot, and the prior was a fixed Beta(2,2).
- `meta_sources.py`: `eff_games` per archetype (Kish n ÷ pilot design effect; τ = 13% pilot spread, `pilot_sd`). Build split into `build_meta_sheet` (network, saves `.meta_store/last_inputs.json`) and `rebuild_meta_sheet(use_weights, sources)` (cache only, ~0.2 s) + `_assemble`.
- `deck_score.py`: **Score** = expected win rate vs today's English-legal field. Hierarchical empirical Bayes (deck strength θ + matchups, μ / k_s / k_m fitted). In-person Day 2 → Top 32 enters through a fitted logit link β (currently ≈ 0.6, i.e. the data shows a weak link, so in-person barely moves θ). Bayesian conversion is shown in the Converts column.
  - **Your decks, two scores:** *with archetype* (a version of the matched archetype, ±4.2-pt deck spread) and *own games only* (typical-deck prior with deck + pilot spread, worth ≈ 12 games).
  - **Opponent strength** (Preferences → Opponent strength, `weights["strength"]`): ladder win rate − `ladder_gap` 4 pts on the log-odds scale, then each ladder game × `ladder_evidence` 0.5 (your Limitless games × `tournament_evidence` 1.0).
  - **Ranking rule (user decision):** ranked once **effective** games ≥ (1.645 × 0.5 / `rank_margin`)² = 30 for ±15 pts. No raw game count. Gengar Testing = 14 games ≈ 7 effective → not ranked yet.
  - Meta decks are ranked among themselves; your decks get "would rank #N". P(#1) / P(top 5) come from joint simulation.
- `formulas.py`: every custom rating / weighting shows its formula **with the real numbers** on hover (estimate / range / chance, game and event weights, effective games, Score terms, in-person link, opponent strength, conversion, Share / Top cut).
